## Rules (so quality stays high)

1. **Specific beats general.** Every list item must be usable by a real couple/friend, not filler.
2. **One CTA per post**, linking to `/create`; one contextual link to the matching landing page.
3. **Internal links:** each post links to 2 other posts ("More guides" is automatic).
4. **Honesty:** only claim features LoveDrop actually has (music, scheduling, 90-day storage, no signup, privacy).
5. **No keyword stuffing** — target keyword appears in title, H1, first paragraph, and one H2.
6. Update the sitemap + `blogPosts.js` (single source of truth), tests auto-cover new posts.
